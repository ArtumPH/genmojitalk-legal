## 🚀 Quick Help

### Getting started
Open the app → Chat tab → tap any emoji to hear it spoken in your
selected language.

### Set a custom name for an emoji
Open the **Synthesizer** tab, build the emoji you want, tap **Name**.
Type a custom phrase (e.g. "my little cat" for 🐱), optionally record
your own voice, then save.

### Voice It (turn a photo or GIF into a voice video)
Open the **Voice It** tab → Pick from Library → choose a photo, GIF,
Live Photo or video → record your voice → Save & Share.

### Voice Tag-Team (chain a voice video with friends)
1. Create a Voice It video and share it with a friend.
2. The friend opens it in GenmojiTalk via "Pick from Files".
3. GenmojiTalk detects the chain and lets them add their own segment
   on top of yours.

### Collection mode (stitch multiple segments into one 60s video)
Open the **Voice It** tab → New Collection → tap "Add segment" to
build up to a 60-second clip.

## 💳 Subscription & Purchase

### What does Premium unlock?
- Unlimited custom names (free tier: 20)
- Unlimited voice recordings (free tier: 5)
- Unlimited favorites (free tier: 10)
- No watermark on Voice It exports
- AI Genmoji custom naming

### Monthly vs Lifetime — which should I pick?
- **Monthly Premium ($4.99/mo)** comes with a 3-day free trial.
  Cancel anytime.
- **Lifetime Premium ($29.99 one-time)** unlocks Premium forever.
  Best value if you plan to use the app for more than 6 months.

### Family Sharing
Both Monthly and Lifetime support Apple Family Sharing. After
purchase, your eligible family members can use Premium too — open
**Settings (iOS) → Apple ID → Family Sharing → Subscriptions** to
enable.

### How do I restore purchases on a new device?
Open the app → **Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases**.
Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to
purchase.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
**Settings (iOS) → Apple ID → Subscriptions → GenmojiTalk →
Cancel Subscription**. Or use the Manage Subscription link inside
the app's Settings → Subscription section.

### I want a refund
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com),
sign in with your Apple ID, find the GenmojiTalk purchase and
request a refund.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

### My voice recording won't play back
- Make sure you've granted Microphone permission in iOS Settings
- Try recording the clip again from My Dictionary
- If a recorded clip is enabled in My Dictionary but missing on
  disk (e.g., after a restore), the app will fall back to the
  built-in system voice

### Genmoji creation isn't available
Genmoji requires iOS 18.0+ on a device that supports Apple
Intelligence. Make sure Apple Intelligence is enabled in iOS
Settings.

### Voice It video is sideways or has black bars
This was fixed in the latest update. Please update to the latest
version. If it still happens, please email us with the source media
type.

### Voice Tag-Team chain isn't detected when I re-open a video
- Make sure the video was imported via **Pick from Files**, not via
  Photos. Apple's Photos app strips custom metadata when re-importing
  videos
- Save the chain video to Files (or iCloud Drive) before re-importing

### Share Extension doesn't open my app automatically
On iOS 26, share extensions can no longer wake the host app. After
sharing to GenmojiTalk, manually open the app — it will detect the
incoming media on next launch.

## 🚀 快速上手

### 入门
打开应用 → 聊天页 → 点击任何 emoji 即可用您选定的语言朗读。

### 给 emoji 设置自定义名称
打开 **合成器** 页，组合您想要的 emoji，点击 **命名**。输入自定义
短语（例如给 🐱 输入"我家小猫"），可选录入您自己的声音，然后保存。

### Voice It（让照片或 GIF 变成会说话的视频）
打开 **Voice It** 页 → 从图库选择 → 选一张照片、GIF、Live Photo
或视频 → 录入您的声音 → 保存并分享。

### Voice Tag-Team（与朋友接龙配音视频）
1. 创建 Voice It 视频并分享给朋友
2. 朋友通过"从文件中选择"在 GenmojiTalk 中打开
3. GenmojiTalk 会检测到接龙并让朋友在您的内容上添加自己的片段

### 合集模式（拼接多段成 60 秒视频）
打开 **Voice It** 页 → 新建合集 → 点击"添加片段"组合最长 60 秒的
短片。

## 🛠 故障排除

### 我的录音无法播放
- 请确保在 iOS 设置中已授予麦克风权限
- 尝试从"我的词典"重新录制
- 如果"我的词典"中某条录音已启用但文件丢失（例如恢复后），应用
  将自动回退到内置系统声音

### 无法创建 Genmoji
Genmoji 需要 iOS 18.0+ 且设备支持 Apple Intelligence。请确保已
在 iOS 设置中启用 Apple Intelligence。

### Voice It 视频侧躺或有黑边
最新版本已修复。请更新到最新版本。如仍出现问题，请发送邮件告知
源媒体类型。

### 重新打开视频时未检测到接龙
- 请确保通过 **从文件中选择** 而非"照片"应用导入。iOS 照片应用
  在重新导入视频时会剥离自定义元数据
- 在重新导入前，请先将接龙视频保存到"文件"（或 iCloud Drive）

### 分享扩展未自动打开 App
在 iOS 26 上，分享扩展无法再唤醒宿主应用。分享到 GenmojiTalk
后，请手动打开应用 — 应用将在下次启动时检测到传入的媒体。
